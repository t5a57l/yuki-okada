Biosketch

I am currently a master's student in the Kuroda Laboratory at The University of Tokyo. Using graded obesity mice, I aim to clarify how metabolic regulation changes as obesity progresses.

I received my B.A. in Environment and Information Studies from Keio University in 2025. During my undergraduate years at Keio University, I worked at the Laboratory for Integrated Cellular Systems, RIKEN. There, I developed a method for estimating key molecules in transcriptional regulatory networks. This project was also selected as an Outstanding Graduation Project at the university.

Since 2023, I have been working as a data scientist at Epistra Inc. I analyze clients' omics data and also develop methods for automatically generating experimental designs. My research interests lie in systems biology, omics data analysis, and data-driven approaches to understanding biological systems.
